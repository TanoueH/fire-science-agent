## Scientific scope

The manuscript evaluates contemporaneous reconstruction at six omitted sites from four observed virtual sites. It uses six development scenarios and three reserved scenarios evaluated once after protocol freeze. It reports mixed method performance and does not claim general ML superiority or a novel inference algorithm. The severity bands are local research criteria, not validated human-safety or operational thresholds.

The earlier multi-agent implementation and its three-scenario materials remain in `src/`, `analysis/`, and existing data paths for provenance. They are not used to generate the current manuscript results. No new simulation, physical observation, metric, or scientific claim was introduced by this repository update.
